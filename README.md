### rudr

Cloud, infra & distributed systems.

I build things. That's most of it — an idea shows up, I want to know how it
breaks, and the fastest way to find out is to build the thing and run it.
Half of these never leave my machine. The other half turn into something.

What keeps pulling me back is failure. A single process is easy. Ten of them
talking over a network, with one of them dead and nobody sure which — that's
the interesting part. Consensus, replication, retries that don't make things
worse, state that survives a restart. I'd rather ship something boring that
stays up than something clever that needs babysitting.

**Building**

- [Indeks](https://github.com/ru-dr/indeks) — privacy-first, self-hosted analytics. Your data stays on your box.
- [Plip](https://github.com/ru-dr/plip) — a logger that's pleasant to read. Small, opinionated, no config ceremony.
- Mycelium — local-first sync engine. Offline writes, conflicts resolved on reconnect. In progress.

**Working with**
